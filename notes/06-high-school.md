## Price Action Trading

### [What is Price Action Trading?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-price-action-trading)
- Trading off raw price movement and structure with few or no indicators.

### [Market Structure](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/market-structure)
- Uptrend = higher highs and higher lows; downtrend = lower highs and lower lows. A break of structure hints at change.

### [Swing Highs and Swing Lows](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/swing-highs-and-swing-lows)
- Turning points that define structure and anchor stops, Fibs and trend lines.

### [Supply and Demand Zones](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/supply-and-demand-zones)
- Areas where price left sharply, suggesting unfilled orders; price often reacts on return.

### [Reading Candles in Context](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/reading-candles-in-context)
- A pin bar at key support means more than one mid range. Location matters more than shape.

### [Price Action Setups](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/price-action-setups)
- Pin bars, engulfing bars, inside bars and breakouts at key levels.

### [Price Action Confluence](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/price-action-confluence)
- Stack factors: level + trend + candle signal + time frame alignment.

### [Price Action Across Multiple Time Frames](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/price-action-across-multiple-timeframes)
- Higher time frame for direction and zones, lower for timing.

### [Summary: Price Action Trading](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/price-action-trading-summary)
- Structure, levels and candles in context form the core toolkit.

## Trading Breakouts and Fakeouts

### [What is a Breakout?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-a-breakout)
- Price moving decisively beyond a level or pattern boundary, often with expanding volatility.

### [Where Breakouts Happen](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/where-breakouts-happen)
- Ranges, triangles, key highs and lows, session ranges, and around major news.

### [Reading the Breakout Candle](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/reading-the-breakout-candle)
- Strong body closing near its extreme beyond the level is healthier than a long wick poke.

### [Confirming Breakouts](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/confirming-breakouts)
- Candle close beyond the level, follow through, rising volatility, alignment with higher time frame.

### [Why Breakouts Fail](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/why-breakouts-fail)
- Thin liquidity, stop hunts, counter trend breaks and lack of catalyst.

### [Break and Retest](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/break-and-retest)
- Wait for price to return to the broken level, which flips role, then enter for a better price and tighter stop.

### [Trading Fakeouts](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/trading-fakeouts-2)
- When price breaks then quickly returns inside the range, trade the reversal with stops beyond the false break.

### [Measuring Targets](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/measuring-targets)
- Project the range or pattern height from the breakout point; use the next major level as a sanity check.

### [Breakout Trading in Context](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/breakout-trading-in-context)
- Breakouts in the direction of the larger trend have better odds.

### [Summary: Breakouts and Fakeouts](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/trading-breakouts-and-fakeouts-summary)
- Confirm, manage risk, and be ready to flip when breaks fail.

## Market Environment

### [Trendspotting](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/trendspotting)
- First job is classifying the market: trending or ranging, and how volatile.

### [What is a Trending Market?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-a-trending-market)
- Sustained directional movement with clear structure; use trend following tools and buy pullbacks.

### [What is a Ranging Market?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-a-ranging-market)
- Price oscillates between support and resistance; use oscillators and fade the extremes.

### [Retracement or Reversal?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/trend-retracement-or-reversal)
- Retracements are temporary pullbacks within a trend; reversals change the trend. Watch structure and fundamentals.

### [Identifying Reversals](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/identifying-reversals)
- Break of trend line or structure, divergence, reversal patterns, and a shift in fundamentals.

### [Protect Yourself From Reversals](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/protect-yo-self-from-reversals)
- Use stops, trail them, and take partial profits.

## Multiple Time Frame Analysis

### [Multiple Time Frame Analysis](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/multiple-time-frame-analysis)
- Looking at the same pair across time frames to align trend, setup and entry.

### [What Time Frame Should I Trade?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-time-frame-should-i-trade)
- Pick based on schedule, personality and patience; shorter frames mean more noise and costs.

### [Why Trade Multiple Time Frames?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/why-trade-multiple-time-frames)
- Avoid trading against the bigger trend and get more precise entries.

### [How Time Frames Differ Across Markets](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-time-frames-differ-across-markets)
- Session effects and liquidity make the same time frame behave differently by market.

### [Time Frame Combinations](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/time-frame-combinations)
- Use roughly a 4 to 6 times ratio: e.g. daily, 4 hour, 1 hour, or 1 hour, 15 minute, 5 minute.

### [Setting Up a Multi Time Frame Workspace](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/setting-up-your-multil-time-frame-workspace)
- Arrange charts so higher frame bias and lower frame trigger are visible together.

### [Time Frame Mashup](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/time-frame-mashup)
- Worked example walking from the long term trend down to the entry chart.

### [Common MTF Mistakes](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/common-mtf-mistakes)
- Too many time frames, conflicting signals, and switching frames to justify a trade.
