## Heikin Ashi

### [What is Heikin Ashi?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-heikin-ashi)
- "Average bar" candles that smooth price to make trends easier to see.

### [Heikin Ashi vs Traditional Candlesticks](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/heikin-ashi-vs-traditional-japanese-candlestick)
- HA candles use averaged values, so they lag and do not show actual open or close prices.

### [How to Calculate Heikin Ashi](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-calculate-heikin-ashi)
- Close = (O+H+L+C)/4; Open = (prior HA open + prior HA close)/2; High = max(H, HA open, HA close); Low = min(L, HA open, HA close).

### [How to Use a Heikin Ashi Chart](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-use-a-heikin-ashi-chart)
- Strings of green candles without lower wicks mean strong uptrend; red without upper wicks mean strong downtrend; small bodies with both wicks mean possible change.

### [How to Trade Heikin Ashi](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-trade-heikin-ashi)
- Use colour changes and wick behaviour for entries and to stay in trends; confirm with support and resistance.

### [Heikin Ashi Limitations](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/heikin-ashi-limitations)
- Lag, hidden gaps and no true prices; place orders off a regular chart.

### [Summary: Heikin Ashi](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/heikin-ashi-summary)
- Great trend filter, poor precision tool.

## Elliott Wave Theory

### [Elliott Wave Theory](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/elliott-wave-theory)
- Markets move in repeating waves driven by crowd psychology, fractal across time frames.

### [The 5-3 Wave Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/the-5-3-wave-patterns)
- Five impulse waves (1 to 5) in the trend direction, then three corrective waves (A, B, C).

### [Corrective Waves](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/corrective-waves)
- Zigzag (5-3-5, sharp), flat (3-3-5, sideways) and triangle (3-3-3-3-3) corrections.

### [Waves Within a Wave](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/waves-within-a-wave)
- Each wave subdivides into smaller waves of the same structure.

### [The 3 Cardinal Rules](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/the-3-cardinal-rules-and-some-guidelines)
- Wave 2 never retraces beyond the start of wave 1; wave 3 is never the shortest impulse; wave 4 does not overlap wave 1 territory.

### [Riding Elliott's Waves](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/riding-elliotts-waves)
- Best opportunity is entering at the end of wave 2 to ride wave 3; use the rules to invalidate counts.

### [Summary: Elliott Wave Theory](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/summary-of-elliott-wave-theory)
- Subjective but useful for framing where the market may be in its cycle.

## Harmonic Price Patterns

### [Harmonic Price Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/harmonic-price-patterns)
- Patterns defined by specific Fibonacci ratios between swings, used to spot potential reversal zones.

### [ABCD and Three-Drive](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/the-abcd-and-the-three-drive)
- ABCD: leg CD equals AB in price and time, with BC retracing 61.8% to 78.6% of AB. Three-drive: three symmetric pushes to an exhaustion point.

### [Gartley and the Animals](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/the-gartley-and-the-animals)
- Gartley (XABCD with D at 78.6% of XA), Bat, Butterfly and Crab variants with different Fib requirements.

### [3 Steps to Trade Harmonic Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/3-steps-in-trading-harmonic-price-patterns)
- Identify the pattern, measure the Fib ratios, then enter at the completion zone with a tight stop.

### [Summary: Harmonic Price Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/summary-of-harmonic-price-patterns)
- Precise rules, but wait for confirmation; patterns fail too.

## Trading Divergences

### [Divergence Trading](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/divergence-trading)
- When price and an oscillator disagree, momentum may be shifting.

### [Regular Divergence](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/regular-divergence)
- Price higher high, indicator lower high: bearish reversal signal. Price lower low, indicator higher low: bullish.

### [Hidden Divergence](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/hidden-divergence)
- Signals trend continuation: price higher low with indicator lower low (bullish), price lower high with indicator higher high (bearish).

### [How to Trade Divergences](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-trade-divergences)
- Connect swing highs or lows on both price and indicator; enter on confirmation like a break of structure.

### [Momentum Tricks](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/momentum-tricks)
- Use divergence to spot fading moves before price turns.

### [9 Rules for Trading Divergences](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/9-rules-for-trading-divergences)
- Use clear swing points, compare matching swings, stay on the same time frame, ignore tiny swings, and wait for confirmation.

### [Divergence Cheat Sheet](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/divergence-cheat-sheet)
- Table of the four divergence types and their implications.

### [Summary: Divergences](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/summary-of-divergences)
- Regular divergence warns of reversals, hidden divergence supports continuation.
