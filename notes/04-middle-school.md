## Oscillators and Momentum Indicators

### [Leading vs Lagging Indicators](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/leading-vs-lagging-indicators)
- Leading indicators try to anticipate moves (more false signals); lagging indicators confirm moves (late entries).

### [Leading Indicators (Oscillators)](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/leading-indicators-oscillators)
- Stochastic, RSI, Williams %R, CCI: bounded oscillators that flag overbought and oversold, best in ranges.

### [Lagging Indicators (Momentum)](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/lagging-indicators-momentum-indicators)
- MAs, MACD, Parabolic SAR: follow trends and keep you in them, but signal after the move starts.

### [Summary: Leading and Lagging Indicators](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/summary-leading-and-lagging-indicators)
- Use oscillators in ranges and momentum tools in trends; know which environment you are in.

## Important Chart Patterns

### [Chart Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/chart-patterns-schmatterns)
- Recurring price formations that hint at continuation or reversal; measured moves give targets.

### [Double Top and Double Bottom](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/double-top-and-double-bottom)
- Two failed tests of a high (top) or low (bottom). Neckline break confirms; target is the pattern height projected from the neckline.

### [Head and Shoulders](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/head-and-shoulders)
- Left shoulder, higher head, lower right shoulder; neckline break signals reversal. Inverse version marks bottoms.

### [Wedges](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/wedges)
- Rising wedge (bearish) and falling wedge (bullish) with converging trend lines sloping the same way.

### [Rectangles](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/rectangles)
- Horizontal consolidation between support and resistance; trade the breakout, target equal to rectangle height.

### [Pennants](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/pennants)
- Small symmetrical consolidation after a sharp move (flagpole); usually breaks in the direction of the pole.

### [Triangles](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/triangles)
- Symmetrical (either way), ascending (bullish bias, flat top) and descending (bearish bias, flat bottom).

### [How to Trade Chart Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-trade-chart-patterns)
- Enter on the breakout or retest, stop inside the pattern, target the measured move.

### [Chart Patterns Cheat Sheet](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/chart-patterns-cheat-sheet)
- Reference table of patterns, bias, entry, stop and target.

## Pivot Points

### [Pivot Points](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/forex-pivot-points)
- Levels calculated from the prior period's high, low and close, widely watched for intraday support and resistance.

### [How to Calculate Pivot Points](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-calculate-pivot-points)
- PP = (H + L + C) / 3. R1 = 2PP − L, S1 = 2PP − H, R2 = PP + (H − L), S2 = PP − (H − L), R3 = H + 2(PP − L), S3 = L − 2(H − PP).

### [Other Pivot Point Calculation Methods](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/other-pivot-point-calculation-methods)
- Woodie's (weights the close), Camarilla (tight levels for mean reversion), Fibonacci pivots (Fib multiples of range).

### [Range Trading With Pivot Points](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/range-trading-with-pivot-points)
- In quiet markets, fade moves to S1/R1 with stops beyond the next level.

### [Breakout Trading With Pivot Points](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/playing-the-breaks-with-pivot-points)
- In active markets, trade the break of a pivot level toward the next one.

### [Using Pivot Points to Gauge Sentiment](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/using-pivot-points-to-determine-market-sentiment)
- Price above PP leans bullish, below leans bearish.

### [Summary: Pivot Points](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/pivot-points-summary)
- Objective levels many traders watch; use for entries, stops, targets and bias.
