## Which Type of Trader Are You?

### [Choosing Your Trading Style](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/choosing-your-trading-style)
- Match holding period to schedule and personality.

### [What is Day Trading?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-day-trading)
- Positions opened and closed within the day; no overnight risk, but needs screen time.

### [What is Scalping?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-scalping)
- Very short trades for a few pips each; costs and execution speed matter most.

### [What is Swing Trading?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-swing-trading)
- Holds for days to weeks to catch swings; fits part-time traders, faces overnight and weekend risk.

### [What is Position Trading?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-position-trading)
- Holds for weeks to months on fundamental themes; needs wide stops and patience.

### [What Type of Trader Are You?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-type-of-trader-are-you)
- Self assessment to pick a style.

### [Quiz: Trading Styles](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/quiz-trading-styles)
- Chapter quiz.

## Create Your Own Trading System

### [What is a Trading System?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-a-trading-system)
- Objective rules for entries, exits and sizing that can be tested.

### [How to Design Your Trading System](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-design-your-trading-system)
- Choose time frame, trend filter, entry trigger, stop, exit and position sizing rules.

### [Test Before You Risk Real Money](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/test-your-system-before-you-risk-real-money)
- Backtest, then forward test on demo or small size; track win rate, expectancy and drawdown.

### [Pullback System](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/pullback-system)
- Example system: trade in the trend direction after a pullback to a moving average or level.

### [Sticking With Your Trading System](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/sticking-with-your-trading-system)
- Every system has losing streaks; abandoning it early ruins the edge.

### [Quiz: Trading System](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/quiz-create-your-own-trading-system)
- Chapter quiz.

## Keeping a Trading Journal

### [Why Traders Need a Journal](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/why-traders-need-a-journal)
- Turns experience into data so you can find what works.

### [Three Layers Every Journal Needs](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/three-layers-every-trading-journal-needs)
- Trade data, reasoning behind the trade, and emotional state.

### [Before You Trade](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/before-you-trade)
- Log the setup, thesis, entry, stop, target and risk before clicking.

### [After You Trade: The Retrospective](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/after-you-trade-the-retrospective)
- Record outcome, whether you followed rules, and lessons.

### [Does Your System Make Money?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/does-your-trading-system-actually-make-money)
- Compute expectancy = (win rate × average win) − (loss rate × average loss).

### [How to Review Your Journal](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-review-your-trading-journal)
- Weekly and monthly reviews by setup, pair, session and mistake type.

### [Keep Your Journal Alive](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/keep-your-trading-journal-alive)
- Keep it simple enough to maintain every trade.

### [Quiz: Trading Journal](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/quiz-keeping-a-trade-journal)
- Chapter quiz.

### [Which Journal Tool Should You Use?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/which-trading-journal-tool-should-you-use)
- Spreadsheet, notebook or dedicated app; pick what you will actually use.
