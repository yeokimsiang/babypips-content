## Risk Management

### [Risk Management Explained](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/risk-management-explained)
- Protecting capital comes first; survival lets an edge play out.

### [Drawdown Explained](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/drawdown-explained)
- Peak-to-trough decline. Recovery needed = 1/(1 − drawdown) − 1: a 50% loss needs a 100% gain.

### [How Much Should You Risk Per Trade?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-much-should-you-risk-per-trade)
- Common guideline: 1% to 2% of equity per trade, less while learning.

### [Risk Budgeting](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/risk-budgeting)
- Cap total risk per day, week and across open trades.

### [Position Sizing: Calculating Trade Size](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/position-sizing-how-to-calculate-your-trade-size)
- Size = (account × risk %) / (stop in pips × pip value per lot).

### [Reward to Risk Ratio and Expectancy](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/reward-to-risk-ratio-and-expectancy)
- Breakeven win rate = 1 / (1 + R:R). Positive expectancy needs the right mix of win rate and R:R.

### [Your Personal Risk Management Plan](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/building-your-personal-risk-management-plan)
- Write down risk per trade, daily stop, max open risk and rules after losing streaks.

- Chapter quiz.

## The Number 1 Cause of Death of Forex Traders

### [Why Leverage Kills Most New Traders](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/why-leverage-kills-most-new-traders)
- Over-leverage turns ordinary moves into account ending losses.

### [Leverage and Margin](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/leverage-and-margin)
- Leverage is a tool; margin is the deposit. More leverage means less room for error.

### [Global Leverage Landscape](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/global-leverage-landscape)
- Regulators cap retail leverage (e.g. 30:1 majors in the EU, UK and Australia, 50:1 in the US); offshore brokers offer far more.

### [The Margin Call](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/the-margin-call)
- When equity falls below required margin, positions get closed at the worst time.

### [Effective vs Offered Leverage](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/effective-leverage-vs-offered-leverage)
- Effective leverage = total position notional / equity. That, not the broker's maximum, is your real risk.

### [Six Leverage Myths](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/six-leverage-myths-that-destroy-accounts)
- Myths like "high leverage means high profits" or "small accounts need high leverage".

### [Prop Firms and Funded Accounts](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/prop-firms-and-funded-accounts)
- Drawdown limits at prop firms force leverage discipline.

### [Using Leverage Without Blowing Up](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-use-leverage-without-blowing-your-account)
- Size by risk, not by available margin; keep effective leverage low.

## Position Sizing

### [What is Position Sizing?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-position-sizing)
- Deciding how many units to trade so each loss stays within your risk limit.

### [Calculating Your Position Sizes](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/calculating-your-position-sizes)
- Worked examples across account currencies and pairs, including crosses and JPY pairs.

### [Volatility Adjusted Position Sizing](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/volatility-adjusted-position-sizing)
- Use ATR based stops so volatile pairs get smaller size and quiet pairs larger.

### [Portfolio Heat](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/portfolio-heat-managing-total-risk-exposure)
- Sum of risk across all open trades; cap it (e.g. 5% to 6%) and watch correlation.

## Setting Stop Losses

### [What is a Stop Loss?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-a-stop-loss)
- A pre-set exit that defines the most you will lose on a trade.

### [Stop Placement](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/stop-placement)
- Place where your trade idea is proven wrong, then size the position to fit.

### [How to Set a Stop Loss](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-set-stop-loss)
- Methods: percentage, chart based (beyond swing or level), volatility based, and time stops.

### [Setting Stops Using Volatility](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-set-stop-using-volatility)
- Stop at a multiple of ATR (e.g. 1.5 to 2 × ATR) to stay outside normal noise.

### [Why Your Stop Keeps Getting Hit](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/why-your-stop-keeps-getting-hit)
- Stops too tight, at obvious levels, or ignoring spread and volatility.

### [Stop Loss Mistakes](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/stop-loss-mistakes)
- Moving stops further away, not using them, and placing them on round numbers.

### [Stop Loss Playbook](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/stop-loss-playbook)
- Pick the method by setup and market environment.

### [Quiz: Stop Losses](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/quiz-setting-stop-losses)

## Scaling In and Out

### [Scaling](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/scaling)
- Adding to or reducing a position in stages rather than all at once.

### [Scaling Out of a Winner](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-scale-out-a-winning-position)
- Take partial profits at targets and trail the rest; trades off maximum gain for consistency.

### [Pyramiding](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/pyramiding)
- Add to winners as the trend confirms, with smaller additions and stops moved up.

### [ATR Based Sizing for Scaling](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/atr-based-sizing-for-scale-in-and-scale-out)
- Space entries and exits by ATR multiples.

### [Scaling Into a Zone](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/scaling-into-a-zone)
- Split entries across a support or resistance zone; total risk still fixed in advance.

### [Scaling Playbook](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/scaling-playbook)
- Rules for when to add, when to reduce, and how to keep total risk capped.

### [Quiz: Scaling](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/quiz-scaling-in-and-out)

## Currency Correlations

### [Currency Correlation Explained](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/currency-correlation-explained)
- Coefficient from −1 to +1 measuring how two pairs move together.

### [How to Read Correlation](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-read-correlation)
- Above +0.7 strong positive, below −0.7 strong negative, near zero unrelated.

### [Pairs That Move Together](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/currency-pairs-that-move-together)
- EUR/USD and GBP/USD positive; EUR/USD and USD/CHF negative.

### [Commodity Currencies and Hidden Drivers](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/commodity-currencies-and-their-hidden-drivers)
- AUD, NZD, CAD share drivers in commodities and risk mood.

### [Cross-Asset Correlations](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/beyond-fx-cross-asset-correlations)
- Links between FX and gold, oil, yields and equities.

### [Correlations Change](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/correlations-change)
- Correlations shift with regimes; check them regularly.

### [Are You Doubling Your Risk?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/are-you-doubling-your-risk)
- Two trades in highly correlated pairs act like one bigger position.

### [Knowing Your True Exposure](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/knowing-your-true-exposure)
- Net out exposure by currency, e.g. long EUR/USD plus short USD/CHF is double short USD.

### [Using Correlations to Avoid Bad Trades](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/using-correlations-to-avoid-bad-trades)
- Use related pairs to confirm or question a setup.

### [When Correlations Break](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/when-correlations-break)
- Breaks often signal a currency specific story or regime shift.

### [How to Calculate Correlations](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-calculate-correlations)
- Pearson correlation on returns over a chosen window, e.g. with a spreadsheet CORREL function.
