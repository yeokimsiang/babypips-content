## The U.S. Dollar Index

### [Global Role of the U.S. Dollar](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/global-role-of-us-dollar)
- Reserve currency, dominant in trade invoicing and funding; most FX trades involve USD.

### [What is the Dollar Index?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-the-dollar-index)
- DXY measures USD against six currencies, weighted heavily to EUR (about 58%), then JPY, GBP, CAD, SEK, CHF.

### [How to Read the Dollar Index](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-read-the-dollar-index)
- Rising DXY = broad dollar strength. Base value 100 from 1973.

### [Using the USDX for Forex](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/using-the-usdx-for-forex)
- Confirm whether a move is dollar driven; pick the pair with the clearest counterpart.

### [Trade Weighted Dollar Index](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/trade-weighted-dollar-index)
- Fed's broad index weights by actual trade partners, including China and Mexico.

### [Bloomberg Dollar Index](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/bloomberg-dollar-index)
- Broader basket weighted by trade and liquidity, including Asian and EM currencies.

### [The Dollar Smile Theory](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/the-dollar-smile-theory)
- USD strengthens both in global crises (safe haven) and when US growth outperforms; it weakens in between.

## Intermarket Correlations

### [What is Intermarket Analysis?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-intermarket-analysis)
- Using bonds, equities and commodities to inform currency views.

### [Risk On, Risk Off and Currencies](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/risk-on-risk-off-how-market-mood-moves-currencies)
- Global risk appetite links stocks, yields and FX in predictable ways.

### [Gold Price Drivers](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/gold-price-drivers)
- Real yields, USD, central bank buying, and safe haven demand.

### [As Gold as It Gets](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/as-gold-as-it-gets)
- Gold often moves inversely to USD; AUD tends to correlate with gold.

### [Black Crack (Oil)](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/black-crack)
- Oil drives CAD, NOK and inflation expectations.

### [The U.S. Dollar and Oil Relationship](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/us-dollar-and-oil-relationship-changing)
- Historically inverse; US shale made the link weaker and sometimes positive.

### [Copper and Industrial Metals](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/copper-and-industrial-metals-what-they-signal-for-currencies)
- Copper tracks global growth and China demand; linked to AUD, CLP, PEN.

### [Silver](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/silver-the-metal-that-cant-make-up-its-mind)
- Part precious, part industrial metal, so it follows both gold and growth.

### [The 411 on Bonds](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/the-411-on-bonds)
- Bond prices and yields move inversely; yield differentials attract capital flows.

### [Yield Curve](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/yield-curve)
- Plot of yields across maturities; slope reflects growth and policy expectations.

### [Yield Curve Inversions](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/yield-curve-inversions)
- Short yields above long yields; historically a recession warning.

### [Bond Spreads](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/bond-spreads)
- Yield gap between two countries' bonds often leads the currency pair.

### [Bonds, Fixed Income and Forex](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/bond-markets-fixed-income-securities-and-the-forex-market)
- Rising relative yields usually support the currency.

### [Equities and Currencies](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/equities-and-currencies-the-big-picture)
- Strong stock markets attract foreign inflows; risk appetite links equities to commodity currencies.

### [Quiz: Intermarket Correlations](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/quiz-intermarket-correlations)
- Chapter quiz.

### [Intermarket Analysis Cheat Sheet](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/intermarket-analysis-cheat-sheet)
- Summary table of key cross-asset relationships.

### [Beta Currencies](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/beta-currencies)
- High beta currencies (AUD, NZD, EM) swing more with global risk; low beta (CHF, JPY) less.

## Country Profiles

### [Guide to Major Economies](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/forex-traders-guide-to-major-economies)
- Know each economy's growth drivers, trade mix, central bank and key data.

### [United States](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/united-states-of-america)
- Services and consumption driven; Fed dual mandate; key data NFP, CPI, PCE, ISM.

### [Euro Zone](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/euro-zone)
- ECB price stability mandate across many members; Germany and France dominate; watch HICP and PMIs.

### [United Kingdom](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/united_kingdom)
- Services and finance heavy; BoE inflation target; GBP sensitive to politics.

### [Japan](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/japan)
- Export powerhouse with long running low rates; JPY is a safe haven and funding currency.

### [Canada](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/canada)
- Commodity and oil exporter closely tied to the US; CAD tracks oil.

### [Australia](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/australia)
- Mining exports to China; AUD is a risk and commodity proxy.

### [New Zealand](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/new-zealand)
- Dairy and agricultural exports; RBNZ; NZD is high beta.

### [Switzerland](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/switzerland)
- Safe haven with big current account surplus; SNB known for intervention.

### [China](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/china)
- Managed CNY with daily fix; demand drives commodity currencies.

## Global Liquidity Analysis for Beginners

### [What is Global Liquidity Analysis?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-global-liquidity-analysis)
- Tracking how much money and credit flows through the system and how it drives asset prices.

### [SOFR vs IORB](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/sofr-vs-iorb-lending-stress)
- When the repo rate (SOFR) trades above interest on reserves (IORB), funding markets are tight.

### [The Fed's Repo Backstop (SRF)](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/fed-repo-backstop-srf)
- Standing Repo Facility lets banks swap Treasuries for cash to cap repo rate spikes.

### [Treasury General Account (TGA)](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/treasury-general-account-tga-explained)
- Government's account at the Fed; a rising TGA drains reserves, a falling one adds liquidity.

### [Stocks, Bonds Doom Loop](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/stocks-bonds-doom-loop)
- Rising yields hit stocks, which tightens conditions further; a feedback loop to watch.

### [DXY as a Liquidity Gauge](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/dollar-index-dxy-key-gauge-global-liquidity)
- A strong dollar tightens global financial conditions; a weak dollar eases them.

### [Gold and Bitcoin as Liquidity Signals](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/gold-bitcoin-liquidity-confirmation-signals)
- Both tend to rise when liquidity expands, confirming the trend.

### [Central Bank Balance Sheets](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/central-bank-balance-sheets-global-liquidity)
- QE expands balance sheets and liquidity; QT shrinks them.

### [How Balance Sheets Impact Forex](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-central-bank-balance-sheets-impact-forex)
- Relatively faster balance sheet growth tends to weaken a currency.

### [Global M2 Money Supply](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/global-m2-money-supply-liquidity-indicator)
- Aggregate broad money across major economies as a liquidity trend indicator.

### [Global Liquidity Cheat Sheet](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/global-liquidity-cheat-sheet)
- Summary of indicators and what rising or falling readings mean.

## Narrative Analysis

### [What is Narrative Analysis?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-narrative-analysis)
- Studying the stories the market tells itself, which drive flows beyond the data.

### [Understanding Market Narratives](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/understanding-market-narratives)
- Narratives simplify complex data into themes like "Fed pivot" or "US exceptionalism".

### [Lifecycle of Market Narratives](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/lifecycle-of-market-narratives)
- Emergence, adoption, consensus, saturation, then exhaustion or reversal.

### [Narratives and Trader Psychology](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-market-narratives-influence-trader-psychology-and-behavior)
- Narratives fuel herding, confirmation bias and FOMO.

### [The Narrative Pipeline](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/narrative-pipline)
- How ideas travel from analysts and media to price action.

### [Using Narrative Analysis in Your Trading](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/using-narrative-analysis-in-your-trading)
- Identify the dominant story, its stage, and what would break it; trade with it early and fade it late.
