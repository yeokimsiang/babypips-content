## Support and Resistance Levels

### [Support and Resistance](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/support-and-resistance)
- Support is a price zone where buying tends to halt declines; resistance is where selling halts rallies.
- Treat them as zones, not exact lines. Broken resistance often becomes support and vice versa.

### [Psychological Levels](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/psychological-level)
- Round numbers (e.g. 1.1000, 150.00) attract orders and often act as support or resistance.

### [Candlesticks With Support and Resistance](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/candlestick_with_support_and_resistance)
- Rejection candles (long wicks, engulfing bars) at a level add confirmation that it is holding.

### [Trading the Lines](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/trading-the-lines)
- Bounce: buy near support or sell near resistance with stops beyond the zone.
- Break: trade the move through a level, ideally after a close beyond it or a retest.

### [Trend Lines](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/trend-lines)
- Connect at least two swing lows (uptrend) or highs (downtrend); a third touch validates. Steeper lines break more easily.

### [Trend Channels](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/trend-channels)
- A parallel line to the trend line forms a channel; buy the lower edge in up channels, sell the upper edge in down channels.

### [Dynamic Support and Resistance](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/dynamic-support-and-resistance)
- Moving averages act as moving support or resistance in trends.

### [Summary: Support and Resistance](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/summary-trading-support-resistance)
- Levels are zones, round numbers matter, and role reversal is common. Confirm with price action.

## Japanese Candlesticks

### [What is a Japanese Candlestick?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-a-japanese-candlestick)
- Body spans open to close; wicks (shadows) show the high and low. Bullish candles close above open.

### [Bodies and Shadows](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/sexy-bodies-and-strange-shadows)
- Long bodies mean strong pressure; short bodies mean indecision. Long upper wicks show rejected highs, long lower wicks rejected lows.

### [Basic Candlestick Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/basic-candlestick-patterns)
- Spinning tops, marubozu (no wicks, full conviction) and doji (open equals close) set the basic vocabulary.

### [Single Candlestick Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/lone-rangers-single-candlestick-patterns)
- Hammer and hanging man (long lower wick), inverted hammer and shooting star (long upper wick). Context, i.e. where in the trend, decides meaning.

### [Dual Candlestick Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/double-trouble-dual-candlestick-patterns)
- Bullish and bearish engulfing, tweezer tops and bottoms: reversal signals after a move.

### [Triple Candlestick Patterns](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/triple-candlestick-patterns)
- Morning and evening star, three white soldiers, three black crows, three inside up and down.

### [Japanese Candlesticks Cheat Sheet](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/japanese-candlesticks-cheat-sheet)
- Quick reference of pattern shapes and their bullish or bearish implications.

### [Candlestick Trading Mistakes](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/japanese-candlestick-trading-mistakes)
- Trading patterns without context, ignoring the higher time frame, and acting before the candle closes.

### [Summary: Candlesticks](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/summary-of-candlesticks)
- Candles show the battle between buyers and sellers; combine with levels and trend for signals.

## Fibonacci

### [Fibonacci Who?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/fibonacci-who)
- Ratios from the Fibonacci sequence: 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6% retracements; 127.2%, 161.8% extensions.

### [Fibonacci Retracement](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/fibonacci-retracement)
- Draw from swing low to swing high in an uptrend (high to low in a downtrend) to find pullback zones to join the trend.

### [When Fibonacci Fails](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/when-fibonacci-fails)
- Price can slice through every level; Fibs are areas of interest, not guarantees. Always use stops.

### [Combining Fibs With Support and Resistance](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/combining-fibs-with-support-and-resistance)
- Confluence of a Fib level with prior support or resistance raises the odds of a reaction.

### [Combining Fibs With Trend Lines](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/combining-fibs-with-trend-lines)
- Look for Fib levels that coincide with an intact trend line.

### [Combining Fibs With Candlesticks](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/combining-fibs-with-candlesticks)
- Wait for a reversal candle at a Fib level as entry confirmation.

### [Fibonacci Extensions](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/fibonacci-extensions)
- Project profit targets beyond the prior swing, e.g. 127.2%, 161.8%.

### [Placing Stops With Fibs](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/placing-stops-with-fibs)
- Stop beyond the next Fib level or beyond the swing point the retracement was drawn from.

### [Summary: Fibonacci](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/summary-of-fibonacci)
- Fibs mark areas, need confluence, and help with entries, stops and targets.

## Moving Averages

### [Moving Averages](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/silky-smooth-moving-averages)
- Average price over N periods smooths noise and reveals trend direction.

### [Simple Moving Averages](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/simple-moving-averages)
- SMA = sum of last N closes / N. Equal weight; slow to react and sensitive to spikes dropping out.

### [Exponential Moving Average](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/exponential-moving-average)
- EMA weights recent prices more, so it reacts faster.

### [SMA vs EMA](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/sma-vs-ema)
- EMA: faster, more false signals. SMA: smoother, more lag. Choose by style.

### [Using Moving Averages](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/using-moving-averages)
- Price above a rising MA suggests an uptrend; MAs also act as dynamic support or resistance.

### [Moving Average Crossover Trading](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/moving-average-crossover-trading)
- Fast MA crossing above slow MA is bullish, below is bearish. Works in trends, whipsaws in ranges.

### [Moving Average Envelopes](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-use-moving-average-envelopes)
- Bands set a fixed percentage above and below an MA to spot overextension.

### [Moving Average Ribbon](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/moving-average-ribbon)
- Many MAs plotted together; fanning out shows trend strength, compression shows consolidation.

### [Guppy Multiple Moving Average](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/guppy-multiple-moving-average)
- Two groups of EMAs (short term traders and long term investors); separation and crossover of the groups signal trend changes.

### [Summary: Moving Averages](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/summary-of-moving-averages)
- MAs smooth price, define trend and offer crossover signals, all with lag.

## Popular Chart Indicators

### [Bollinger Bands](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/bollinger-bands)
- MA (usually 20) with bands at ±2 standard deviations. Squeeze signals low volatility before breakouts; band touches in ranges can mean reversion.

### [BBTrend](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/bbtrend-by-john-bollinger)
- Bollinger's indicator comparing short and long band widths to measure trend strength and direction.

### [Average True Range (ATR)](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/atr)
- Average of the true range over N periods; measures volatility, not direction. Used to size stops and positions.

### [Keltner Channel](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/keltner-channel)
- EMA with bands at a multiple of ATR; smoother than Bollinger. Closes outside suggest trend strength.

### [VWAP](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-use-volume-weighted-average-price-vwap)
- Volume weighted average price for the session; institutions use it as a fair value benchmark. In FX it relies on tick volume.

### [MACD](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/macd)
- MACD line = 12 EMA − 26 EMA; signal = 9 EMA of MACD; histogram = difference. Crossovers and zero line crosses signal momentum shifts.

### [Parabolic SAR](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/parabolic-sar)
- Dots trailing price that flip on reversals; useful for trailing stops in trends, poor in ranges.

### [Stochastic](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/stochastic)
- Compares close to the recent range (0 to 100). Above 80 overbought, below 20 oversold; %K and %D crossovers.

### [Relative Strength Index (RSI)](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/relative-strength-index)
- Momentum oscillator 0 to 100, typically 14 periods. Above 70 overbought, below 30 oversold; also used for divergence and 50 line trend bias.

### [Williams %R](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/williams-r)
- Like an inverted stochastic, scaled 0 to −100; above −20 overbought, below −80 oversold.

### [Average Directional Index (ADX)](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/average-directional-index)
- Measures trend strength regardless of direction; above 25 trending, below 20 weak. +DI and −DI give direction.

### [Choppiness Index](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/choppiness-index-chop)
- High values mean sideways chop, low values mean trending. Helps choose trend or range tactics.

### [Ichimoku Kinko Hyo](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/ichimoku-kinko-hyo)
- Tenkan, kijun, two cloud spans and chikou span. Price above the cloud is bullish; cloud acts as support or resistance.

### [Best Technical Indicator for Forex?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/best-technical-indicator-forex)
- No single best indicator; choose a few that fit your strategy and avoid clutter.

### [Putting It All Together](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/putting-it-all-together)
- Combine a trend tool, a momentum tool and a volatility tool rather than several of the same kind.

### [Summary: Common Chart Indicators](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/summary-common-chart-indicators)
- Indicators derive from price, so they confirm rather than predict. Know what each measures.
