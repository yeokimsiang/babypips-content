## What is Forex?

### [What is Forex?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-forex)
- Forex (FX) is the global, decentralised market for exchanging one currency for another.
- Retail traders speculate on price changes between two currencies; nothing physical is delivered.

### [What is Traded in Forex?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-traded-in-forex)
- You always trade currencies in pairs: buying one means selling the other.
- Majors all include USD (EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD, USD/CHF, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, NZD/USD). Crosses exclude USD; exotics pair a major with an emerging market currency.

### [Buying and Selling Currency Pairs](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/buying-selling-currency-pairs)
- Quote format BASE/QUOTE: the price is how much quote currency buys one unit of base.
- Go long (buy) if you expect the base to strengthen; go short (sell) if you expect it to weaken.

### [Market Size and Liquidity](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/market-size-and-liquidity)
- FX is the largest financial market, with daily turnover in the trillions of USD, concentrated in USD pairs and in London and New York.
- High liquidity means tight spreads and easy execution in the majors, less so in exotics.

### [Different Ways to Trade Forex](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/different-ways-to-trade-forex)
- Retail access routes: spot FX, CFDs, currency futures, options, ETFs and spread betting (in some countries).
- Each differs in leverage, costs, regulation and whether there is a central exchange.

## How to Trade Forex

### [How Do You Make Money Trading Forex?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/make-money-trading-forex)
- Profit comes from the exchange rate moving in your favour after you open a position.
- Worked logic: buy EUR/USD, euro strengthens, you close higher and keep the difference.

### [Bid and Ask Price Explained](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/bid-and-ask-price-explained)
- Bid is the price you can sell at; ask (offer) is the price you can buy at.
- Ask is always above bid; the gap is the spread.

### [What is a Spread?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-a-spread-in-forex-trading)
- Spread = ask minus bid, the built-in cost of entering a trade.
- Spreads widen around news, at session rollover and in illiquid pairs.

### [Pips and Pipettes](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/pips-and-pipettes)
- A pip is the standard unit of price movement: 0.0001 for most pairs, 0.01 for JPY pairs. A pipette is one tenth of a pip.
- Pip value = (pip size / exchange rate) × position size, in the base currency; convert to account currency as needed.

### [Lots, Leverage, and Profit and Loss](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/lots-leverage-and-profit-and-loss)
- Standard lot = 100,000 units, mini = 10,000, micro = 1,000, nano = 100. On USD-quoted pairs a standard lot is about $10 per pip.
- P&L = pips moved × pip value × number of lots; leverage magnifies it both ways.

### [Margin vs Leverage](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/margin-vs-leverage)
- Margin is the deposit the broker holds to keep a position open; leverage is the ratio of position size to that deposit.
- Margin requirement 2% ⇔ 50:1 leverage. Leverage = 1 / margin requirement.

### [Time to Make Some Dough](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/time-to-make-some-dough)
- Walkthrough of a simple trade from entry to exit, showing how a pip move becomes dollars.
- Reinforces that losses scale exactly like gains.

### [Types of Forex Orders](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/types-of-orders)
- Market order fills now at the best price. Limit entry buys below or sells above the market; stop entry buys above or sells below.
- Stop loss caps loss; take profit locks gains; trailing stop follows price; GTC and GFD set order duration; OCO links two orders so one cancels the other.

### [Order Types Cheat Sheet](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/order-types-cheat-sheet)
- Quick reference table of order types and when to use each.

### [Demo Your Way to Success](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/demo-your-way-to-success)
- Practise on a demo account first to learn the platform and test a method without risking cash.
- Demo fills and emotions differ from live trading, so move to small live size once consistent.

### [Impress Your Date With Forex Lingo](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/impress-your-date-with-forex-lingo)
- Glossary of trader slang: cable (GBP/USD), fiber (EUR/USD), loonie (CAD), aussie, kiwi, swissie, gopher (USD/JPY).
- Terms like long, short, bull, bear, and the "figure".

### [Can Forex Trading Make You Rich?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/can-forex-trading-make-you-rich)
- Most retail traders lose money; consistent profits need skill, risk control and time.
- Treat it as a skill to develop, not a get rich quick scheme.

## When Can You Trade Forex?

### [Forex Trading Sessions](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/forex-trading-sessions)
- FX trades 24 hours a day, five days a week, across Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York sessions.
- Liquidity and volatility depend on which centres are open.

### [Tokyo Session](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/can-trade-forex-tokyo-session)
- Asian session: JPY, AUD and NZD pairs are most active; ranges are often narrower.
- Good for range strategies; Japanese exporters and data can move JPY.

### [London Session](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/london-session)
- The busiest centre by volume; trends often start here and volatility is highest in the majors.
- EUR, GBP and CHF pairs are most active.

### [New York Session](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/new-york-session)
- US data releases and equity market open drive USD pairs.
- Liquidity drops after London closes, typically mid New York afternoon.

### [Session Overlaps](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/session-overlaps)
- London and New York overlap is the most liquid and volatile window of the day.
- Tokyo and London overlap is thinner but can see breakouts from the Asian range.

### [Best Days of the Week to Trade](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/best-days-of-the-week-to-trade)
- Midweek (Tuesday to Thursday) tends to be most active; Monday can be slow and Friday erratic into the weekend.
- Holidays and big news days change the usual rhythm.

## Who Trades Forex?

### [Forex Market Structure](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/forex-market-structure)
- OTC, decentralised market with a tiered structure: interbank at the top, then institutions, then retail brokers.
- No single exchange price; quotes vary slightly by venue.

### [Forex Market Players](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/market-players)
- Central banks, big commercial banks, hedge funds and asset managers, corporations hedging trade flows, and retail traders.
- Banks and large funds move the market; retail is a small share.

### [Know Your Forex History](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/know-your-history)
- From the gold standard to Bretton Woods (USD pegged to gold) to floating rates after 1971.
- Electronic trading opened FX to retail participants.

## Why Trade Forex?

### [Advantages of Forex](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/advantages-of-forex)
- 24/5 access, deep liquidity, low costs in majors, leverage, and easy long or short positions.
- Few instruments to follow compared with thousands of stocks.

### [Forex vs Stocks](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/forex-vs-stocks)
- FX: fewer instruments, higher leverage, around the clock, no short-selling restrictions.
- Stocks: centralised exchanges, company fundamentals, lower leverage.

### [Forex vs Futures](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/forex-vs-futures)
- Futures trade on exchanges with standardised contracts, visible volume and commissions.
- Spot FX offers flexible position sizes and no expiry dates.

## Margin Trading 101

### [What is Account Balance?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-account-balance)
- Balance is cash in the account excluding open positions' floating P&L.
- It only changes when trades close or funds move in or out.

### [What is Unrealized Profit or Loss?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-unrealized-profit-or-loss)
- Floating P&L of open positions, marked to current prices.
- Becomes realised when you close.

### [What is Margin?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-margin)
- Collateral set aside to open a leveraged position.
- Required margin = position notional × margin requirement.

### [What is Used Margin?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-used-margin)
- Total margin locked up by all open positions.

### [What is Equity?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-equity)
- Equity = balance + floating P&L. It is the real-time value of the account.

### [What is Free Margin?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-free-margin)
- Free margin = equity − used margin; what you have left to open new trades or absorb losses.

### [What is Margin Level?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-margin-level)
- Margin level = equity / used margin × 100%. Brokers use it to trigger warnings and liquidations.

### [What is a Margin Call Level?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-a-margin-call-level)
- The margin level at which the broker warns you; you can no longer open new positions.

### [What is a Stop Out Level?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-is-a-stop-out-level)
- The margin level at which the broker starts closing positions automatically, usually largest loser first.

### [Margin Call Level and No Stop Out Level Example](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/margin-call-level-and-no-stop-out-level-example)
- Worked example where a broker only has a margin call level; positions close when the call level is hit.

### [Margin Call Level and Stop Out Level Example](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/margin-call-level-and-stop-out-level-example)
- Worked example with separate call and stop out levels, tracking balance, equity and free margin as price moves.

### [Trade Forex With $100](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/trade-forex-with-100-dollars)
- With a tiny account, use micro or nano lots; a standard lot would wipe it out with a small move.
- Shows how fast high leverage can trigger a stop out.

### [Brokers Have Different Margin Call and Stop Out Levels](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/brokers-have-different-margin-call-and-stop-out-levels)
- Levels vary by broker and jurisdiction; check yours before trading.

### [Margin Cheat Sheet](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/margin-cheat-sheet)
- Formula summary: equity, used margin, free margin, margin level, call and stop out.

### [How to Avoid a Margin Call](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-avoid-margin-call)
- Use low effective leverage, stop losses and sensible position size; keep free margin as a buffer.
