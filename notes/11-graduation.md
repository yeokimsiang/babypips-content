## Prop Trading Firms 101

### [What Are Prop Trading Firms?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/what-are-prop-trading-firms)
- Retail prop firms sell evaluations; pass and you trade firm capital for a profit split.

### [Myth vs Reality](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/myth-vs-reality-prop-firms)
- Most traders fail evaluations; "funded" accounts are often simulated.

### [How Prop Firms Make Money](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-prop-firms-make-money)
- Mainly challenge fees and resets; payouts are funded by the fees of those who fail.

### [The Prop Firm Ecosystem](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/prop-firm-industry-ecosystem)
- Firms, technology providers, brokers and liquidity partners behind the scenes.

### [Are Prop Firms a Scam?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/are-prop-firms-a-scam)
- Not inherently, but the model has conflicts; read rules and payout terms carefully.

### [Are All Prop Firms Scams?](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/are-all-prop-firms-scams)
- Quality varies; check track record, payout proof and transparency.

### [How to Succeed With Prop Firms](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/how-to-succeed-with-prop-firms)
- Treat it like a real account: small risk, consistency, and respect drawdown rules.

### [Prop Firm Risk Management](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/prop-firm-risk-management)
- Daily and max drawdown limits dictate per-trade risk far below the limits.

### [Risk Management Plan for Prop Traders](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/risk-management-plan-prop-traders)
- Set a personal daily stop tighter than the firm's, and scale risk with buffer.

### [Risk Management Mistakes in Challenges](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/risk-management-mistakes-prop-challenges)
- Over-sizing to pass fast, revenge trading, ignoring news restrictions.

### [Advanced Prop Risk Techniques](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/prop-firm-risk-management-advanced-techniques)
- Dynamic sizing based on distance to drawdown limit and equity curve.

### [Prop Firms and White Label Tech](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/prop-firms-and-white-label-tech-providers)
- Many firms run on the same white label platforms; know who actually holds the risk.

### [Prop Firm Safety Guide](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/prop-firm-safety-guide)
- Checklist to vet a firm before paying.

### [Emotional Triggers That Blow Accounts](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/emotional-triggers-blow-prop-firm-accounts)
- Pressure of rules and fees triggers overtrading and tilt.

### [The Future of Prop Trading Firms](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/future-of-prop-trading-firms)
- Regulation and consolidation are reshaping the industry.

### [Build Your Prop Firm Strategy](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/build-your-prop-firm-strategy)
- Choose a strategy that fits the firm's rules and time limits.

### [Challenge Survival Guide](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/prop-firm-challenge-survival-guide)
- Practical routine for getting through an evaluation.

### [Truth About Retail Prop Firms](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/truth-about-retail-prop-firms)
- They sell a product; the edge must still be yours.

## Forex Trading Scams

### [Forex Account Managers](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/forex-account-managers)
- Be wary of anyone offering to manage your money for guaranteed returns.

### [Don't Be a Sucker](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/dont-be-a-sucker)
- If returns sound too good to be true, they are.

### [Automated Robots and Systems](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/automated-robots-and-systems)
- Most EAs sold online are curve-fit or fake; demand verified live results.

### [Signal Services](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/signal-services)
- Many sell hype; verify track records independently.

### [Some Brokers Are Scammers Too](https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/some-brokers-are-scammers-too)
- Unregulated brokers can block withdrawals or manipulate prices.
