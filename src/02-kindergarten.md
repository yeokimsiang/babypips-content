# Kindergarten {kindergarten}

## Choosing a Forex Broker {choosing-a-broker}

### How to Choose a Forex Broker {how-to-choose-forex-broker}
- Check regulation first, then costs (spread, commission, swaps), execution quality, platform, funding options and support.

### How Forex Brokers Work {how-forex-brokers-work}
- Retail brokers are your counterparty or route orders to liquidity providers; they earn from spreads, commissions and sometimes client losses.

### Is Your Forex Broker Legit or a Scam? {forex-broker-legit-or-scam}
- Verify the licence on the regulator's own register, look for segregated funds and transparent pricing.
- Red flags: guaranteed returns, withdrawal delays, pressure selling.

### Regulated vs Unregulated Forex Brokers {regulated-vs-unregulated-forex-brokers}
- Regulators (e.g. FCA, ASIC, NFA/CFTC, MAS) enforce capital rules, client fund protection and leverage caps.
- Offshore unregulated brokers offer high leverage but little recourse.

### What Retail Forex Traders Trade {what-retail-forex-traders-trade}
- Retail traders usually trade derivatives (rolling spot or CFDs), not physical currency delivery.

### Trading Forex CFDs {trade-forex-cfds}
- A CFD pays the price difference between open and close; overnight positions incur swap or financing charges.

### Where Retail Forex Traders Trade {where-retail-forex-traders-trade}
- Through a broker's platform (MT4/MT5, cTrader, proprietary); the broker sits between you and the interbank market.

### How Brokers Manage Risk and Make Money {how-forex-brokers-manage-risk-make-money}
- Brokers either keep client risk in house, hedge it externally, or mix both depending on client profile.

### B-Book Forex Brokers {b-book-forex-brokers}
- The broker takes the other side of client trades and profits when clients lose; a conflict of interest that needs regulation.

### A-Book Forex Brokers {a-book-forex-brokers}
- Client orders are passed to external liquidity providers; the broker earns markups or commissions.

### How A-Book Brokers Make Money {how-a-book-forex-brokers-make-money}
- Revenue from spread markup, commissions and sometimes rebates from liquidity providers.

### Challenges of A-Book Execution {challenges-of-a-book-execution}
- Higher costs, slippage, and dependence on liquidity providers' willingness to fill.

### STP Forex Brokers {stp-forex-brokers}
- Straight through processing routes orders automatically to liquidity providers without a dealing desk.

### Internalization by Forex Brokers {internalization-by-forex-brokers}
- Broker nets offsetting client orders internally and only hedges the residual exposure.

### Why Forex Brokers B-Book {why-forex-brokers-b-book}
- Most retail clients lose, so keeping their flow is profitable and cheaper than routing it out.

### Hybrid Forex Broker Model {hybrid-forex-broker-model}
- Profitable or large clients are A-booked; small, unprofitable flow is B-booked.

### C-Book Forex Brokers {c-book-forex-brokers}
- Partial or strategic hedging of client flow, sometimes trading against or alongside it.

### Know Your Broker's Hedging Policy {know-forex-brokers-hedging-policy}
- Ask how the broker handles your orders; transparency on execution model matters for trust.

### Where Do Forex Broker Prices Come From? {forex-brokers-price-come}
- Prices are aggregated from liquidity providers, then marked up; quotes differ between brokers.

### Order Execution Quality {order-execution-quality}
- Judge brokers on fill speed, slippage (positive and negative), requotes and rejections.

## Three Types of Market Analysis {three-types-of-analysis}

### Fundamental Analysis {fundamental-analysis}
- Analyse economic, political and social forces driving currency supply and demand.

### What is Fundamental Analysis? {what-is-fundamental-analysis}
- Uses data like interest rates, growth, inflation and employment to judge a currency's value.

### Sentiment Analysis {sentimental-analysis}
- Gauges the market's overall mood and positioning; price reflects what traders believe, not just facts.

### The Big Three {the-big-three}
- Technical, fundamental and sentiment analysis together give a fuller picture.

### Technical Analysis {technical-analysis}
- Studies price history on charts to find patterns and probabilities; assumes price discounts known information.

### Which Type of Analysis is Best? {which-type-of-analysis-is-best}
- None is best on its own; combine them to suit your style and time frame.

## Types of Charts {types-of-charts}

### Types of Forex Charts {types-of-charts}
- Line chart (closes only), bar chart (OHLC), and candlestick chart (OHLC with coloured bodies).

### Chart Types: Strengths and Weaknesses {trading-chart-types-strengths-weaknesses}
- Lines show the big picture cleanly; bars and candles show intraperiod range; candles are easiest to read.
