## Prop Trading Firms 101 {prop-trading-firms-101}

### What Are Prop Trading Firms? {what-are-prop-trading-firms}
- Retail prop firms sell evaluations; pass and you trade firm capital for a profit split.

### Myth vs Reality {myth-vs-reality-prop-firms}
- Most traders fail evaluations; "funded" accounts are often simulated.

### How Prop Firms Make Money {how-prop-firms-make-money}
- Mainly challenge fees and resets; payouts are funded by the fees of those who fail.

### The Prop Firm Ecosystem {prop-firm-industry-ecosystem}
- Firms, technology providers, brokers and liquidity partners behind the scenes.

### Are Prop Firms a Scam? {are-prop-firms-a-scam}
- Not inherently, but the model has conflicts; read rules and payout terms carefully.

### Are All Prop Firms Scams? {are-all-prop-firms-scams}
- Quality varies; check track record, payout proof and transparency.

### How to Succeed With Prop Firms {how-to-succeed-with-prop-firms}
- Treat it like a real account: small risk, consistency, and respect drawdown rules.

### Prop Firm Risk Management {prop-firm-risk-management}
- Daily and max drawdown limits dictate per-trade risk far below the limits.

### Risk Management Plan for Prop Traders {risk-management-plan-prop-traders}
- Set a personal daily stop tighter than the firm's, and scale risk with buffer.

### Risk Management Mistakes in Challenges {risk-management-mistakes-prop-challenges}
- Over-sizing to pass fast, revenge trading, ignoring news restrictions.

### Advanced Prop Risk Techniques {prop-firm-risk-management-advanced-techniques}
- Dynamic sizing based on distance to drawdown limit and equity curve.

### Prop Firms and White Label Tech {prop-firms-and-white-label-tech-providers}
- Many firms run on the same white label platforms; know who actually holds the risk.

### Prop Firm Safety Guide {prop-firm-safety-guide}
- Checklist to vet a firm before paying.

### Emotional Triggers That Blow Accounts {emotional-triggers-blow-prop-firm-accounts}
- Pressure of rules and fees triggers overtrading and tilt.

### The Future of Prop Trading Firms {future-of-prop-trading-firms}
- Regulation and consolidation are reshaping the industry.

### Build Your Prop Firm Strategy {build-your-prop-firm-strategy}
- Choose a strategy that fits the firm's rules and time limits.

### Challenge Survival Guide {prop-firm-challenge-survival-guide}
- Practical routine for getting through an evaluation.

### Truth About Retail Prop Firms {truth-about-retail-prop-firms}
- They sell a product; the edge must still be yours.

### Some Brokers Are Scammers Too {some-brokers-are-scammers-too}
