## The U.S. Dollar Index {dollar-index}

### Global Role of the U.S. Dollar {global-role-of-us-dollar}
- Reserve currency, dominant in trade invoicing and funding; most FX trades involve USD.

### What is the Dollar Index? {what-is-the-dollar-index}
- DXY measures USD against six currencies, weighted heavily to EUR (about 58%), then JPY, GBP, CAD, SEK, CHF.

### How to Read the Dollar Index {how-to-read-the-dollar-index}
- Rising DXY = broad dollar strength. Base value 100 from 1973.

### Using the USDX for Forex {using-the-usdx-for-forex}
- Confirm whether a move is dollar driven; pick the pair with the clearest counterpart.

### Trade Weighted Dollar Index {trade-weighted-dollar-index}
- Fed's broad index weights by actual trade partners, including China and Mexico.

### Bloomberg Dollar Index {bloomberg-dollar-index}
- Broader basket weighted by trade and liquidity, including Asian and EM currencies.

### The Dollar Smile Theory {the-dollar-smile-theory}
- USD strengthens both in global crises (safe haven) and when US growth outperforms; it weakens in between.

## Intermarket Correlations {intermarket-correlations}

### What is Intermarket Analysis? {what-is-intermarket-analysis}
- Using bonds, equities and commodities to inform currency views.

### Risk On, Risk Off and Currencies {risk-on-risk-off-how-market-mood-moves-currencies}
- Global risk appetite links stocks, yields and FX in predictable ways.

### Gold Price Drivers {gold-price-drivers}
- Real yields, USD, central bank buying, and safe haven demand.

### As Gold as It Gets {as-gold-as-it-gets}
- Gold often moves inversely to USD; AUD tends to correlate with gold.

### Black Crack (Oil) {black-crack}
- Oil drives CAD, NOK and inflation expectations.

### The U.S. Dollar and Oil Relationship {us-dollar-and-oil-relationship-changing}
- Historically inverse; US shale made the link weaker and sometimes positive.

### Copper and Industrial Metals {copper-and-industrial-metals-what-they-signal-for-currencies}
- Copper tracks global growth and China demand; linked to AUD, CLP, PEN.

### Silver {silver-the-metal-that-cant-make-up-its-mind}
- Part precious, part industrial metal, so it follows both gold and growth.

### The 411 on Bonds {the-411-on-bonds}
- Bond prices and yields move inversely; yield differentials attract capital flows.

### Yield Curve {yield-curve}
- Plot of yields across maturities; slope reflects growth and policy expectations.

### Yield Curve Inversions {yield-curve-inversions}
- Short yields above long yields; historically a recession warning.

### Bond Spreads {bond-spreads}
- Yield gap between two countries' bonds often leads the currency pair.

### Bonds, Fixed Income and Forex {bond-markets-fixed-income-securities-and-the-forex-market}
- Rising relative yields usually support the currency.

### Equities and Currencies {equities-and-currencies-the-big-picture}
- Strong stock markets attract foreign inflows; risk appetite links equities to commodity currencies.

## Country Profiles {country-profiles}

### Guide to Major Economies {forex-traders-guide-to-major-economies}
- Know each economy's growth drivers, trade mix, central bank and key data.

### United States {united-states-of-america}
- Services and consumption driven; Fed dual mandate; key data NFP, CPI, PCE, ISM.

### Euro Zone {euro-zone}
- ECB price stability mandate across many members; Germany and France dominate; watch HICP and PMIs.

### United Kingdom {united_kingdom}
- Services and finance heavy; BoE inflation target; GBP sensitive to politics.

### Japan {japan}
- Export powerhouse with long running low rates; JPY is a safe haven and funding currency.

### Canada {canada}
- Commodity and oil exporter closely tied to the US; CAD tracks oil.

### Australia {australia}
- Mining exports to China; AUD is a risk and commodity proxy.

### New Zealand {new-zealand}
- Dairy and agricultural exports; RBNZ; NZD is high beta.

### Switzerland {switzerland}
- Safe haven with big current account surplus; SNB known for intervention.

### China {china}
- Managed CNY with daily fix; demand drives commodity currencies.

## Global Liquidity Analysis for Beginners {global-liquidity-analysis-for-beginners}

### What is Global Liquidity Analysis? {what-is-global-liquidity-analysis}
- Tracking how much money and credit flows through the system and how it drives asset prices.

### SOFR vs IORB {sofr-vs-iorb-lending-stress}
- When the repo rate (SOFR) trades above interest on reserves (IORB), funding markets are tight.

### The Fed's Repo Backstop (SRF) {fed-repo-backstop-srf}
- Standing Repo Facility lets banks swap Treasuries for cash to cap repo rate spikes.

### Treasury General Account (TGA) {treasury-general-account-tga-explained}
- Government's account at the Fed; a rising TGA drains reserves, a falling one adds liquidity.

### Stocks, Bonds Doom Loop {stocks-bonds-doom-loop}
- Rising yields hit stocks, which tightens conditions further; a feedback loop to watch.

### DXY as a Liquidity Gauge {dollar-index-dxy-key-gauge-global-liquidity}
- A strong dollar tightens global financial conditions; a weak dollar eases them.

### Gold and Bitcoin as Liquidity Signals {gold-bitcoin-liquidity-confirmation-signals}
- Both tend to rise when liquidity expands, confirming the trend.

### Central Bank Balance Sheets {central-bank-balance-sheets-global-liquidity}
- QE expands balance sheets and liquidity; QT shrinks them.

### How Balance Sheets Impact Forex {how-central-bank-balance-sheets-impact-forex}
- Relatively faster balance sheet growth tends to weaken a currency.

### Global M2 Money Supply {global-m2-money-supply-liquidity-indicator}
- Aggregate broad money across major economies as a liquidity trend indicator.

### Global Liquidity Cheat Sheet {global-liquidity-cheat-sheet}
- Summary of indicators and what rising or falling readings mean.

## Narrative Analysis {narrative-analysis}

### What is Narrative Analysis? {what-is-narrative-analysis}
- Studying the stories the market tells itself, which drive flows beyond the data.

### Understanding Market Narratives {understanding-market-narratives}
- Narratives simplify complex data into themes like "Fed pivot" or "US exceptionalism".

### Lifecycle of Market Narratives {lifecycle-of-market-narratives}
- Emergence, adoption, consensus, saturation, then exhaustion or reversal.

### Narratives and Trader Psychology {how-market-narratives-influence-trader-psychology-and-behavior}
- Narratives fuel herding, confirmation bias and FOMO.

### The Narrative Pipeline {narrative-pipline}
- How ideas travel from analysts and media to price action.

### Using Narrative Analysis in Your Trading {using-narrative-analysis-in-your-trading}
- Identify the dominant story, its stage, and what would break it; trade with it early and fade it late.
