## Which Type of Trader Are You? {which-type-of-trader-are-you}

### Choosing Your Trading Style {choosing-your-trading-style}
- Match holding period to schedule and personality.

### What is Day Trading? {what-is-day-trading}
- Positions opened and closed within the day; no overnight risk, but needs screen time.

### What is Scalping? {what-is-scalping}
- Very short trades for a few pips each; costs and execution speed matter most.

### What is Swing Trading? {what-is-swing-trading}
- Holds for days to weeks to catch swings; fits part-time traders, faces overnight and weekend risk.

### What is Position Trading? {what-is-position-trading}
- Holds for weeks to months on fundamental themes; needs wide stops and patience.

### What Type of Trader Are You? {what-type-of-trader-are-you}
- Self assessment to pick a style.

## Create Your Own Trading System {create-your-own-trading-system}

### What is a Trading System? {what-is-a-trading-system}
- Objective rules for entries, exits and sizing that can be tested.

### How to Design Your Trading System {how-to-design-your-trading-system}
- Choose time frame, trend filter, entry trigger, stop, exit and position sizing rules.

### Test Before You Risk Real Money {test-your-system-before-you-risk-real-money}
- Backtest, then forward test on demo or small size; track win rate, expectancy and drawdown.

### Pullback System {pullback-system}
- Example system: trade in the trend direction after a pullback to a moving average or level.

### Sticking With Your Trading System {sticking-with-your-trading-system}
- Every system has losing streaks; abandoning it early ruins the edge.

## Keeping a Trading Journal {keeping-a-trading-journal}

### Why Traders Need a Journal {why-traders-need-a-journal}
- Turns experience into data so you can find what works.

### Three Layers Every Journal Needs {three-layers-every-trading-journal-needs}
- Trade data, reasoning behind the trade, and emotional state.

### Before You Trade {before-you-trade}
- Log the setup, thesis, entry, stop, target and risk before clicking.

### After You Trade: The Retrospective {after-you-trade-the-retrospective}
- Record outcome, whether you followed rules, and lessons.

### Does Your System Make Money? {does-your-trading-system-actually-make-money}
- Compute expectancy = (win rate × average win) − (loss rate × average loss).

### How to Review Your Journal {how-to-review-your-trading-journal}
- Weekly and monthly reviews by setup, pair, session and mistake type.

### Keep Your Journal Alive {keep-your-trading-journal-alive}
- Keep it simple enough to maintain every trade.

### Which Journal Tool Should You Use? {which-trading-journal-tool-should-you-use}
- Spreadsheet, notebook or dedicated app; pick what you will actually use.
